PrivacyGuard — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 8, 2026

🛡️ At a Glance: PrivacyGuard performs 100% of its detection locally in your browser. Your text never leaves your device. We never see, collect, or store anything you type or paste.

1. Introduction

PrivacyGuard ("the Extension", "we", "our") is a Chrome browser extension that detects and redacts sensitive data (API keys, credit cards, emails, etc.) before you send it to AI chat tools. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

2. Data Collection — We Collect NOTHING

PrivacyGuard does not collect, transmit, or store any user data.

  • Your text content: All PII detection (regex matching, Luhn validation) runs entirely in your browser. Your pasted or typed text never leaves your device.
  • Detected PII: Sensitive data matches stay in your browser's memory and are discarded when you close the tab.
  • No analytics: We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase, or any third-party analytics service.
  • No telemetry: We do not send crash reports, usage statistics, or diagnostics of any kind.
  • No cookies: The Extension does not set or read any cookies.
  • No accounts: You do not need to create an account or provide any personal information to use PrivacyGuard.

3. Local Storage

The Extension uses Chrome's built-in storage API to save your preferences locally:

  • Which PII detection types are enabled/disabled
  • Your scan scope preference (AI sites only vs. all websites)
  • Daily scan count (to enforce the free tier limit of 5 scans/day)
  • Aggregate statistics (total scans performed, total PII items blocked)

All of this data is stored exclusively on your device. None of it is transmitted to any server. You can clear it at any time by uninstalling the Extension.

4. Third-Party Services

PrivacyGuard does not use any third-party services at runtime.

  • No AI APIs are called
  • No cloud servers are contacted
  • No CDN resources are loaded
  • No external fonts, images, or scripts are fetched

The Extension code is entirely self-contained. Once installed, it operates completely offline.

5. Permissions

PrivacyGuard requests only one permission:

  • Storage: Used solely to save your detection preferences and daily scan count locally in your browser. No data is synced to any external server.

The Extension may request host permissions to inject its content script on websites where you may paste sensitive data. This is required for the core functionality (monitoring paste events). No data is extracted from these pages.

6. Pro License (Future)

If you purchase a Pro license in the future, we may process your license key and payment transaction through a third-party payment processor (e.g., Stripe, Lemon Squeezy). The payment processor will collect billing information as described in their respective privacy policies. We do not store your payment details.

7. Children's Privacy

PrivacyGuard is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date. If we ever change our data handling to collect information (which we currently do not), we will notify users prominently within the Extension before the change takes effect.
