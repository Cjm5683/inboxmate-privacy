The leak everyone hits

Put your API key in front-end JavaScript and anyone can open DevTools, copy it, and spend your money. A leaked Claude key can burn hundreds of dollars overnight.

The fix in one file

The Worker holds the key server-side, checks a shared secret, restricts origins, and rate-limits by IP. Your browser only ever sees your own endpoint.

You own everything

Your code, your Cloudflare bill, your logs. Swap models, enable streaming, tune limits. Nothing here talks to a third-party vendor.

Deploy in about ten minutes

Cloudflare Workers free tier is enough to start.

  1. Unzip the kit. Run npm i -g wrangler && wrangler login.
  2. Put your provider key in a secret: wrangler secret put UPSTREAM_API_KEY.
  3. Edit wrangler.toml: set routes to your hostname and add your domain's origins to the CORS allow-list.
  4. Set your shared secret: wrangler secret put SHARED_SECRET.
  5. Deploy: wrangler deploy. The output shows your Worker URL.
  6. Point your front end at that URL and pass the shared secret in a header.
POST https://your-worker.your-domain.workers.dev/v1/messages
X-Auth-Secret: <your shared secret>

{"model":"claude-sonnet-4","messages":[{"role":"user","content":"hello"}]}
Before you ship: never put the shared secret in front-end JavaScript either. Put it behind a login, or have the browser call your own backend. The Worker stops the API key leak; it can't stop you from leaking the secret.

FAQ

Which providers does it support?

Claude, DeepSeek and GPT out of the box. The upstream call is a normal HTTP request, so OpenAI-compatible and other providers work too — CUSTOMIZE.md shows how to change the URL, headers and body shape.

Does it cost anything?

The Worker runs on Cloudflare's free tier for this kind of traffic. You pay your AI provider directly. There is no middleman and no subscription.

Can I use this in a commercial product?

Yes. MIT license permits commercial use, modification and redistribution. You are not required to credit anything, though a link back is welcome.

Why is it free?

Because it is a small, self-contained piece of engineering and most people need it once. If it saved you an afternoon, grab it and go.

What is the browser extension next to this?

PrivacyGuard is a Chrome extension that stops you pasting API keys and other secrets into AI chatbots. Different problem, same idea: keep the key on your side of the wire.
