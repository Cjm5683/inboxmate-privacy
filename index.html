InboxMate Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 6, 2026

1. Introduction

InboxMate ("the Extension", "we", "our") is a Chrome browser extension that provides AI-powered email assistance within Gmail. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use InboxMate.

2. Data We Collect

We do NOT collect, store, or log your email content on our servers.

The Extension processes data as follows:

  • Email Content: When you use "Draft Reply" or "Summarize Thread", the selected email text is transmitted to a third-party AI service provider (DeepSeek) solely for AI inference. The AI provider processes this data and returns a generated response. We do not store, log, or retain your email content on any server.
  • Contacts & Reminders: Contact names, email addresses, and follow-up reminders you create are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's built-in storage API. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to our servers.
  • Usage Data: A daily count of AI actions you've used is tracked locally and via an anonymous identifier to enforce the free tier limit (5 actions/day). This identifier is a random string generated on installation and cannot be linked to your identity.

3. Third-Party Services

InboxMate uses the following third-party services to provide its functionality:

  • DeepSeek (深度求索): AI model provider. When you request an AI action, your selected email content is sent to DeepSeek's API for processing. DeepSeek's privacy policy is available at platform.deepseek.com/privacy.
  • Cloudflare: Our API proxy runs on Cloudflare Workers. Cloudflare may process IP addresses and request metadata for security and abuse prevention. Cloudflare's privacy policy is available at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

4. Data Storage

All personal data (contacts, reminders, settings) is stored exclusively in your browser's local storage. You can delete this data at any time by:

  • Using the "Clear All Data" button in the Extension's Account tab, or
  • Uninstalling the Extension from Chrome

5. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except as described in Section 3 (AI processing and infrastructure). Your email content is only shared with the AI provider for the purpose of generating responses and is not retained by us.

6. Security

We implement multiple layers of security to protect your data:

  • All data transmission uses HTTPS encryption
  • API requests require client authentication
  • Rate limiting prevents abuse
  • Email content is never logged or stored on our servers
  • No user accounts or passwords are required

7. Your Rights

Since InboxMate stores data locally on your device:

  • You can export your data at any time via the "Export My Data" button
  • You can delete all data via "Clear All Data"
  • Uninstalling the Extension permanently removes all stored data
  • You are in full control of which emails you choose to process with AI

8. Children's Privacy

InboxMate is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
